# Report outline

Title the report `Journey Into PROJECT` and adapt its length to the amount of
meaningful history. Write a connected technical narrative supported by
observation IDs and timestamps.

Cover the sections that the evidence supports:

1. **Project genesis** — initial problem, first implementation, and founding
   technical choices.
2. **Architectural evolution** — major structural changes, their triggers, and
   later consequences.
3. **Key breakthroughs** — moments when investigation turned into a working
   solution.
4. **Work patterns** — debugging cycles, feature bursts, refactoring, and
   exploration.
5. **Technical debt** — shortcuts, why they were accepted, and whether they were
   repaid.
6. **Challenges** — sustained debugging, dead ends, and platform-specific work.
7. **Memory and continuity** — concrete cases where recalled context affected
   later work.
8. **Timeline statistics** — date range, observation/session counts, observation
   types, and meaningful activity patterns.
9. **Lessons** — recurring engineering principles and useful context for a new
   contributor.

Connect events across time and distinguish the timeline's recorded facts from
your interpretation. Do not invent motivations, savings, or causal links. Note
gaps when the history does not support a requested section.
